**Assumptions:** `T0` = first contact with the apple in the bowl. Durations are approximate. Offsets are relative to `T0`. Natural/material processes are placed in a parallel lane at matching offsets.  
**Notation:** `[offset, temporal type, duration]`. Temporal types: `seq`, `parallel`, `cyclical`, `iterative`, `delayed`, `state`, `lingering`.  
**Leaf definition:** a leaf is atomic at this resolution — either an indivisible muscle/joint/contact event or an indivisible physicochemical step. Parent durations are approximate; parallel branches are not additive.
