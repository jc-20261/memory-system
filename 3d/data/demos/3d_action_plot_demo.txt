## 2. ACTION PLOT

### LANE H — Human actions

- `[T0, seq, ~2 s]` Take apple from bowl.
  - `[T0+0.2, parallel, ~2 s]` Feel cold, tight/waxy skin, weight/density under thumb.
- `[T0+2, seq, ~8 s]` Turn apple under tap; run palm over entire surface.
  - `[T0+2, parallel, ~8 s]` Feel stem dimple/crater and faint scar line.
- `[T0+10, seq, ~4 s]` Dry apple on shirt.
- `[T0+14, seq, ~2 s]` Set apple on cutting board, stem-side up; press down.
  - `[T0+14, parallel]` Apple does not roll.
- `[T0+16, seq, ~2 s]` Hold paring knife in right hand, thumb braced on bolster; left hand pins apple, fingertips curled back.
- `[T0+18, seq, ~1 s]` First cut: sink blade just beside stem, ~1/4 inch deep, until pop through skin into flesh.
- `[T0+19, cyclical, ~35–45 s]` Peel: rotate apple against blade; left thumb pushes fruit forward in small increments; knife stays nearly still.
  - `[T0+19, parallel/cyclical]` Peel ribbon forms: continuous, ~1 inch wide, curls away, drops in loose spiral onto board.
  - `[T0+~50, seq, ~5 s]` At bottom, peel narrows; lift knife and reposition twice around calyx end.
- `[T0+~55, seq, ~2 s]` Turn naked apple in hand.
- `[T0+~57, seq, ~3 s]` Quarter apple: blade down through core, firm crunch; second cut; four pieces fall apart.
- `[T0+~60, iterative/cyclical, ~30 s]` For each quarter: lay flat and slice out core.
  - `[T0+~60, repeated 4×]` Extract triangular core wedge with 3 seeds in papery pockets.
- `[T0+~90, seq, ~2 s]` Flick seeds into sink; they click against steel.
- `[T0+~92, seq, ~5 s]` Put slices into bowl.
- `[T0+~97, seq, ~2 s]` Put peels into compost crock.
- `[T0+~99, lingering/parallel, hours]` Hands smell like apple for rest of morning, even after washing.

### LANE N — Natural / material processes in parallel

- `[T0, state, continuous]` Apple is cold from refrigerator; dense with water.
- `[T0+2, parallel, ~8 s]` Water beads and rolls off waxy skin immediately during washing.
- `[T0+10, parallel, ~4 s]` Drying on shirt changes skin from glossy to dull matte red.
- `[T0+18, parallel, immediate]` Cut breaks skin; exposed flesh is pale yellow-white; thin juice film weeps immediately.
- `[T0+19, parallel, ~35–45 s]` Peel separation exposes wet, faintly green-white flesh.
- `[T0+29, delayed, +10 s after first exposure, ongoing]` Exposed flesh begins dulling toward tan; oxidation/browning repeats for every new cut surface.
- `[T0+19, parallel, continuous]` Aroma volatiles rise — green, sharp, faintly floral.
- `[T0+19, parallel]` Blade makes soft, wet, rasping sound, like paper tearing slowly. *(material/acoustic response)*
- `[T0+~55, parallel/after peeling]` Juice runs down wrist to heel of palm.
- `[T0+~55 to +minutes, delayed]` Juice dries tacky on skin.
- `[T0+~57, parallel]` Quartering produces firm crunch; core wedge is fibrous, pale, slightly translucent.
- `[T0+~90, parallel]` Seeds click against steel sink.
- `[T0+~97, parallel]` Peel edges in compost crock are already browning.
- `[T0+~99, lingering, hours]` Apple odor persists on hands after washing.
